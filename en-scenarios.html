THESE ARE NOT PREDICTIONS

Strategic scenarios for the Strait

A structured way to think about the future without claiming certainty. Each scenario combines impact, observable signals and factors that could defuse it.

Qualitative likelihood is not a forecast. It is a revisable editorial assessment with no official status.

SCENARIO 1BASELINE

Stable cooperation

Spain and Morocco contain crises, maintain controls and prioritise commerce, security and shared projects.

Impact
Moderate
Signals
Normalised border, bilateral meetings, operational ferries and customs
SCENARIO 2WATCH

Managed tension

Rhetoric, border delays or limited crises without a breakdown in structural cooperation.

Impact
Medium
Signals
Public accusations, migration pressure, reinforced controls
SCENARIO 3HIGH IMPACT

Prolonged border crisis

Ceuta or Melilla face sustained pressure, overwhelmed services and political conflict within the EU.

Impact
High
Signals
Repeated mass arrivals, crossing closures, extraordinary deployment
SCENARIO 4HIGH IMPACT

Maritime or military incident

A major collision, sabotage, temporary blockage, naval incident or threat to critical infrastructure.

Impact
Very high
Signals
Navigation warnings, diversions, sharp traffic drop, deployments
SCENARIO 5EXTREME

Sovereignty crisis

An explicit escalation over Ceuta or Melilla alters relations, alliances and corridor security.

Impact
Extreme
Signals
Ultimatums, mobilisation, diplomatic rupture or official policy shifts
SCENARIO 6TRANSFORMATION

Deeper integration

A fixed link, new logistics chains and port cooperation turn the Strait into a more integrated economic region.

Impact
Structural
Signals
Investment decision, conclusive studies, long-term agreements
SIGNALS TO WATCH

What would change our assessment

  • Abnormal changes in maritime or ferry traffic.
  • Notices from Tarifa Traffic, Tangier Traffic, Salvamento Marítimo or IMO.
  • Changes in border controls and police cooperation.
  • Official statements on Ceuta and Melilla.
  • Military movements or infrastructure closures.
  • Decisions on ports, customs, the tunnel or energy.
