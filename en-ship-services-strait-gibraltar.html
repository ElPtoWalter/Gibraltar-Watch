PORTS AND MARITIME ECONOMY

Vessel services in the Strait of Gibraltar

An informative guide to bunkering, ship agency, repair, pilotage, towage, supplies and assistance across the port system surrounding the Strait.

WHY HEREROUTE + PORTS + ANCHORAGES

Concentrated traffic and the proximity of Algeciras, Gibraltar, Ceuta and Tangier Med create demand for technical, operational and logistics services during planned calls or incidents.

01

Bunkering

Marine-fuel supply at berth or anchorage, under specific safety and environmental procedures.

02

Ship agency

Documentary and operational coordination of a port call: authorities, crew, cargo, providers and departure.

03

Ship repair

Afloat interventions, maintenance, inspections, workshops, equipment and specialised technical support.

04

Pilotage and towage

Assistance with port manoeuvres and operations requiring specialised support.

05

Ship supplies

Provisions, spares, water, lubricants, consumables and crew support.

06

Emergency and rescue

Coordinated response to breakdowns, pollution, navigation risk or assistance to people.

BEFORE CONTRACTING

What an operator should verify

  • Provider authorisation and actual scope.
  • Availability, operational window and delivery point.
  • Environmental, safety and documentation requirements.
  • Price, currency, taxes, terms and responsibilities.
  • Vessel compatibility and coordination with the agent.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Maritime services

What is bunkering?

It is the supply of fuel to a vessel at berth or anchorage, subject to port, safety and environmental rules.

What does a ship agent do?

The agent represents and assists the vessel and owner during a port call, coordinating formalities and services.

Does Gibraltar Watch recommend companies?

No. The page explains service categories and links to official directories; it does not rank or certify providers.

Where are these services contracted?

Through port authorities, official directories, agents and authorised providers. Conditions should be checked directly.
