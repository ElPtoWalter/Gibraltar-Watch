COOPERATION AND COMPETITION

Spain and Morocco: interdependence under tension

The two countries cooperate on trade, migration, security, energy and major projects. At the same time, they compete for port influence, retain territorial disagreements and experience periodic crises.

THEY COOPERATE

Shared interests

  • Migration control and action against smuggling networks.
  • Bilateral trade and industrial supply chains.
  • Security and counter-terrorism cooperation.
  • Ferries, ports, customs and mobility.
  • The 2030 World Cup and a possible fixed link.
THEY COMPETE

Friction points

  • Algeciras versus Tanger Med.
  • Ceuta and Melilla.
  • Western Sahara and relations with Algeria.
  • The ability to influence migration flows.
  • Regional influence and access to Europe.

THE PARADOX

The more they need each other, the more leverage each can possess

Interdependence reduces the incentive for a complete rupture, but creates levers: borders, customs, police cooperation, trade, ports, fisheries, energy and diplomacy.

Prudent reading

A strategic relationship is not the same as full trust. Nor does every crisis prove a territorial plan.
