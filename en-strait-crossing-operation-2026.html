HOW TO READ THE DASHBOARD

Dated data, not a queue forecast

The daily report describes completed boardings. Access conditions, timetables, availability and waiting times can change after publication.

  • Check the ferry operator and port before travelling.
  • The report date matters as much as the figure.
  • Gibraltar Watch preserves the previous valid data if the source is unavailable.

Primary source

Spanish Secretariat-General for Civil Protection and Emergencies.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

OPE 2026

When does OPE 2026 run?

The official campaign runs from 15 June to 15 September 2026.

Are the figures real time?

No. The page shows the latest official report located and always states its date.

Which Strait routes are shown?

The dashboard highlights Algeciras–Tangier Med, Tarifa–Tangier Ville and Algeciras–Ceuta, alongside other routes in the official report.

Can I use this page to make travel decisions?

Travellers should consult their ferry company, port and Civil Protection. Gibraltar Watch does not manage sailings.
