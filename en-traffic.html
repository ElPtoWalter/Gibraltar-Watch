SEARCH QUESTIONS

What the map can and cannot prove

Does the AIS radar count every vessel?

No. It is an external visualisation and may contain delays, filters or coverage gaps. A visible vessel may be anchored rather than crossing.

What is GIBREP?

It is the mandatory reporting system for certain vessels crossing the Strait, coordinated by Tarifa Traffic and Tangier Traffic within the traffic-separation scheme.

Would an empty map prove the Strait is closed?

No. Missing public AIS signals are never treated as proof of closure. Operational data and official confirmation would be required.

Where can I see ferry and passenger data?

During OPE 2026, Gibraltar Watch summarises official Civil Protection reports and always links to the original document.
